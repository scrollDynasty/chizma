# Idea validation notes

## Hypothesis

Small business owners in Uzbekistan can get a usable website faster by **drawing** it than by picking blocks in Wix/Tilda-like builders.

## What the test must show

1. **Recognition works on real scribbles**: on 20 test sketches, at least 80% of drawn objects become a sensible element.
2. **Time to first page**: under 2 minutes from empty canvas to a preview a user is happy with.
3. **Cost per generation** stays under $0.01 with the default model.
4. **People want it**: 5 of 10 interviewed owners say they would use it for a real site.

## Interview questions

- How did you make your current site (or why don't you have one)?
- Draw your ideal homepage on paper in one minute. What did you draw first?
- What must the site *do*: take orders, show a menu, collect phone numbers?
- Which language do your customers read: Uzbek (Latin/Cyrillic), Russian, both?
